# Timer Sound Effects

This directory contains audio files for Level 1 quiz timer sound effects.

## Files:
- tick.mp3 - Timer tick sound (plays every second)
- warning.mp3 - Warning sound (plays at 5 seconds)
- end.mp3 - End sound (plays when timer reaches 0)

## Usage:
These sounds are automatically loaded and played during the quiz timer countdown to provide audio feedback to users.
